Privacy Policy

Effective October 13, 2026

Overview

This policy explains how MatchlessWorks handles information when you use Rooted & Rising and its related webpages. The app does not require a Rooted & Rising account.

Information you provide

Reflections, goals, journal entries, theme preferences, onboarding status, and limited in-app event history are stored locally on your device unless a feature clearly states otherwise. When you use an AI-guided conversation, your selected topic, messages, and recent conversation context are transmitted securely so a response can be generated. If you contact support, we receive the information included in your message.

Purchases and subscriptions

Apple processes purchases. RevenueCat manages subscription status and purchase restoration and may process transaction and subscription information, product identifiers, an app-specific identifier, country or region, and limited device or app information. MatchlessWorks does not receive complete payment-card information.

AI services

AI-guided conversations are processed through Google Gemini to generate requested responses, operate the feature, maintain safety, and troubleshoot the service. AI responses may be inaccurate and are not medical, mental-health, legal, or emergency advice. Avoid submitting highly sensitive information.

Use and sharing

We use information to provide AI conversations, operate subscriptions, restore purchases, respond to support, protect the service, prevent misuse, troubleshoot problems, and comply with law. We share information only with providers needed to operate the app—including Apple, RevenueCat, hosting providers, and Google Gemini—or when directed or legally required. We do not sell personal information or use it for third-party targeted advertising.

Retention, deletion, and choices

Local data remains on your device until you delete it or remove the app. Providers may retain limited operational records under their policies. You may choose not to use AI conversations, delete local entries, and manage subscriptions through your Apple ID. For deletion of support records or information held by MatchlessWorks, contact us.

Children, health, and emergencies

The app is not directed to children under 13. Rooted & Rising is educational, faith-based self-help content and is not a healthcare provider. If you may harm yourself or someone else, call emergency services; in the United States, call or text 988.

Security and changes

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, but no system is completely secure. We may update this policy as the app changes and will post a revised effective date.
